# Validation report

Mechanical audit of the synopsis and the interim deck against `results/person1/*.json`, the arXiv and Crossref APIs, and the university template. Generated by `scripts/audit_documents.py`; nothing was uploaded to a third party.

## 0. Scope and method

Audited: `docs/SYNOPSIS_interim_draft_paper.docx` (27 references, 5 tables) and `docs/Interim_Project_Presentation_7th-sem.pptx` (15 slides).

- **References** were re-resolved independently through the Crossref API (DOIs) and the arXiv API (preprint identifiers). Title, first author and year were compared with what the document claims; a one-year gap is tolerated because a paper may be cited by journal year while its preprint v1 is earlier.
- **Numbers** were recomputed from `results/person1/*.json` and checked to appear verbatim in the documents, so a figure cannot drift from the evidence that produced it.
- **Conference venues** for NeurIPS/ICLR/ICML papers are stated from the authors' published record: those proceedings carry no DOIs and neither Crossref nor OpenAlex indexes them, so the venue itself is human-attested while titles, authors, years and identifiers are machine-verified.
- Nothing was uploaded to a third party. The installed `verify-citations` skill routes documents through the Stipple API; that was declined for unpublished work, and its checking logic (resolve, compare, report as coverage rather than truth) was applied locally instead.
